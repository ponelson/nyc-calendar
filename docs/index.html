City by the Day

Dance, concerts, opera, talks, Broadway, museums, jazz rooms, and the places that open once a year. Read nightly from the venues’ own listings; everything else links straight to the box office.

SunMonTueWedThuFriSat

Listings are read nightly from structured data the venues publish (APIs, JSON-LD, iCalendar) or, for the jazz rooms, from the NYC Jazz Guide aggregator. Seasons that publish no feed — Park Avenue Armory, the Met’s exhibitions — are hand-curated with dates checked against the venue. Events you add are kept in this browser only.
